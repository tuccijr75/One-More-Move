## V2 joint planner

Explicit Node-only enemyPolicy tactical-v2. Standard is unchanged as the experimental control; Hard and Hardcore enumerate joint destination assignments (at most five choices per enemy and six enemies, 15,625 combinations before pruning). Preserve kill-first and per-enemy best available non-kill BFS chase tier and distance. A stay option permits conflict resolution. Reject duplicate destinations before scoring. Prioritize immediate kill, number of closing moves, then number of moving enemies. Hard prioritizes occupied exits, then threatened exits and depth-two projected coverage; Hardcore prioritizes threatened exits, depth-three projected coverage, then occupied exits. Legacy coordination score is a final tiebreaker. An exit is threatened when an active enemy would be at most one cardinal step away. Projected tiles are reachable through wall-free cardinal movement up to the horizon, not a full adversarial search. Occupied squares count even if an enemy remains stunned; only enemies with no remaining stun count as active threats. No new state fields, RNG draws, movement speed or enemy count changes. Existing collision resolver remains authoritative.

This deliberately replaces v1's explicit role allocation with collective destination optimization. Roles may be revisited after comparative evidence; there is no claim that flanking or containment labels themselves make the AI stronger.
