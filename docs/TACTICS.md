## V2 joint planner

Explicit Node-only enemyPolicy tactical-v2. Standard is unchanged as the experimental control; Hard and Hardcore enumerate joint destination assignments (at most five choices per enemy and six enemies, 15,625 combinations before pruning). Preserve kill-first and per-enemy best available non-kill BFS chase tier and distance. A stay option permits conflict resolution. Reject duplicate destinations before scoring. Prioritize immediate kill, number of closing moves, then number of moving enemies. Hard prioritizes occupied exits, then threatened exits and depth-two projected coverage; Hardcore prioritizes threatened exits, depth-three projected coverage, then occupied exits. Legacy coordination score is a final tiebreaker. An exit is threatened when an active enemy would be at most one cardinal step away. Projected tiles are reachable through wall-free cardinal movement up to the horizon, not a full adversarial search. Occupied squares count even if an enemy remains stunned; only enemies with no remaining stun count as active threats. No new state fields, RNG draws, movement speed or enemy count changes. Existing collision resolver remains authoritative.

This deliberately replaces v1's explicit role allocation with collective destination optimization. Roles may be revisited after comparative evidence; there is no claim that flanking or containment labels themselves make the AI stronger.

## Proposed promotion criteria

Require invariant and baseline gates, useful geometry differentiation, monotonic tactical-player survival and reasonable portal progression without reducing Standard playability. Use held-out seeds before adoption; treat censored observations separately. If the joint search improves movement but fails difficulty separation, inspect equal-distance constraints and propose a canon-compatible trap advantage rule rather than silently weakening chase priority. Full gameplay/device checks, Roblox parity and owner approval precede promotion. Portal scoring, legacy tuning semantics and wall-rule wording conflict remain outside this experiment.

## V2 held-out receipt and V3 experiment

Run 46006189832 at 8761b4c2d877f881081a597e6803fb93688ee3f1: held-out evaluation completed successfully. Tactical-player median observed turns 25/15/15; threshold reach counts at turns 15,30,50,80 were Standard 74/46/15/5, Hard 53/24/6/4, Hardcore 51/24/6/5 out of 100 each, with no earlier censored observations at those thresholds. Zero observed unreachable portals. Standard matched all 500 controls. V2 Hard is the leading candidate; Hardcore is not sufficiently differentiated.

V3 is a Node-only refinement of the same joint planner, not another engine. Preserve v2 exactly for Standard and Hard, and retain v2 as a laboratory reference. For Hardcore only, score wall-aware potential threats to ordinary cardinal continuations two steps ahead. The lexicographic containment proxy minimizes safe first-step exits, maximum safe continuation count, then total safe continuations. A first-step portal is treated as a stage escape, not a dead end; occupied destinations remain illegal. Potential future threats use wall-aware reachability from proposed enemy destinations and ignore future enemy conflicts and Focus abilities, so the score is not a proof of unavoidable death. No new movement distance, spawn rate, cooldown, state fields or RNG changes.

The strict v2 assignment is computed in the same bounded search. V3 can select an equal-closing, equally moving assignment with strictly better containment; or permit at most one off-best chase choice (finite BFS distance at most two greater than the best closing candidate), only if at least one other enemy closes, closing count decreases by at most one, total moving count does not decrease, no immediate kill is available, and the containment proxy strictly improves over the strict plan. Kill always dominates. This is a proposed trap-advantage exception under rules §§2.1/12, NOT an approved change to gameplay canon. Maximum five choices per enemy, six enemies. Keep all shipping paths on baseline.

Run npm run lab:containment: compare v2 vs v3 on fresh seeds 20001–20100, 3,000 runs, same strategy/difficulty matrix and censor-aware thresholds. Standard and Hard must match exactly on observed outcomes and geometry. Retain complete v2 and v3 rows plus source provenance, compare Hardcore both with v2 and with preserved Hard.
